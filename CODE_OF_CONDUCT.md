# Code of conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Welcome people of different backgrounds and levels of experience. Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks, harassment, discrimination or unwanted personal attention.

Do not share another person's private information without permission. If you experience or witness a problem, contact the repository owner privately at [support@retrodarts.com](mailto:support@retrodarts.com). The maintainer may remove content or restrict participation to keep the project welcoming.
