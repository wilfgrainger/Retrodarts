Privacy Policy

Retro Darts Scorer · Last updated 10 November 2026

This policy explains how the Retro Darts Scorer web app handles information.

Information in the app

You can score without an account, and the app works offline. Signing in with Google is optional. The app does not send your matches to the publisher, and it has no advertising or app server. Two features use Google: sign-in with backup to your own Google Drive, and Google Analytics (only if you accept it). Both are described below.

Team names, match settings, scores, the current match, and saved match history are stored in your browser’s local storage and IndexedDB for retrodarts.com. They stay on this device and in this browser profile, and the app never sends them to the publisher. If you turn on Google Drive backup, a copy of your match history and theme is also kept in your own Google Drive, as described below.

The app uses this information locally to provide the scoring and match-history features you choose. The publisher’s lawful basis for this local processing is that it is necessary to provide the app functionality you have chosen to use (performance of the app agreement).

The app does not request your name, email address, contacts or location to score a match.

Google sign-in and Drive backup

Sign in with Google is in Settings. The app loads Google's sign-in code only when you open it. You can tap Start a match without signing in: matches finished that way stay on that device only and are never backed up, even if someone signs in on it later. If the device has no signal, you can score and sign in later. When you sign in, Google asks you to let the app use a private app folder in your Google Drive and to share your email address with the app. The app then copies your match history, theme and settings (vibration, checkout hints, keeping the screen on, and your default match, which includes the two team names you last used) to a file in that private app folder and merges it on your other devices when you sign in there. Each backed-up match is labelled with your Google account identifier, so that on a shared device it is only shown to you and only backed up to your Drive. When you delete a match, the file keeps a note of its identifier and the time it was deleted, so it is also removed from your other devices. Google calls this the app's private data folder: only Retro Darts Scorer can read or write it, and you can see how much space it uses, or delete it, in Google Drive under Settings, Manage apps. Your Drive file stays in your Google account: the publisher never receives or stores it, and the app cannot see your other Drive files.

Cast to TV. If you tap Cast to TV, the app loads Google's Cast code and asks your Chromecast to open the scoreboard page at retrodarts.com/tv. The app then sends that TV the team names and scores shown on screen, through Google Cast on your local network. Nothing is sent until you tap Cast to TV, and the publisher never receives it. Google handles the connection under the Google Privacy Policy.

Your email address is kept only in this browser, to show who is signed in. Your Google account identifier is kept in this browser and in your own Drive file, as described above. Signing in is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. The lawful basis is that this is needed to provide the backup and sync you ask for by signing in. You can stop it at any time by choosing Sign out in the menu or by removing the app's access at myaccount.google.com/connections. To delete the backup, remove the app's access in your Google account.

Retro Darts Scorer's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Analytics and cookies

On your first visit a banner asks whether you allow Google Analytics. If you accept, Google Analytics sets cookies and records which pages you visit, your approximate location, device and browser, and app events such as starting a match, finishing one, downloading a PDF, signing in and changing theme. Team names and scores are never sent. The publisher uses this to understand how the app is used and to improve it. The lawful basis is your consent.

If you reject analytics, no analytics cookies are set and Google Analytics is not loaded. You can change your choice at any time under Cookie settings in the menu. Google processes analytics data under the Google Privacy Policy; Google Analytics keeps event data for up to 2 months.

The app also stores your theme, cookie choice and sign-in status in your browser's local storage. These are needed for the app to work and are not used for tracking.

Exports and sharing

You can download match reports as PDF. Your browser handles these downloads. If you later choose to share a PDF, the service or app you choose handles it under its own privacy terms.

Storage, deletion, and device loss

Completed fixtures stay in this browser’s local history until you delete them or clear the site’s browser data. The current fixture is also stored locally and can be replaced when you start a new match. The app asks the browser to preserve its storage when a match starts, but the browser may decline and storage can still be cleared or evicted. Without Google Drive backup, another browser, browser profile or device will not show these matches, and device loss, browser removal or clearing site data may permanently remove them. With backup on, signing in again restores them from your Drive. You can also download a PDF copy of any match.

Support messages

If you email support@retrodarts.com, the publisher receives your email address, message, and any other details you choose to include. They are used to respond to your support request. Cloudflare Email Routing forwards the message to the publisher’s Google Gmail inbox. Cloudflare and Google are global services and may process information outside the UK; their privacy information and privacy policy describe their handling and transfer arrangements. Do not include sensitive information that is not needed to answer your request.

The publisher’s lawful basis for handling support messages is legitimate interests: responding to support requests and keeping a limited record of the response.

Resolved support emails will be deleted 12 months after resolution, unless they need to be kept longer to handle a further request or meet a legal obligation.

Your data protection rights

You can contact the publisher at support@retrodarts.com to ask about personal information in a support message. Depending on the circumstances, you may have the right to request access to it, correct it, ask for it to be erased or restricted, object to processing based on legitimate interests, or receive it in a portable form where that right applies. These rights are subject to legal conditions.

You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Children and teenagers

The app is intended for people aged 13 and older. It does not ask for a user’s age or send match information to the publisher. If a user emails support, the support-message handling described above applies.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s information practices change, this policy will be updated before the changed version is released. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.

Privacy questions or support: support@retrodarts.com.
